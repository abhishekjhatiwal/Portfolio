1 Overview & Core Privacy Principles

  • Local-First Processing: Documents Hive is designed with a privacy-first, local-first architecture. Your documents, scanned pages, PDFs, and edits are parsed, processed, converted, and stored locally on your device.
  • No Selling of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or documents to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • Minimal Data Collection: We collect only the minimum data required to provide authentication, user profile management, and app functionality.

2 Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in to DocHive (via email/password or Google Sign-In), we collect:
    • Your email address
    • Your full name / display name
    • Your unique account identifier (User ID)
  • Profile Photo: If you choose to upload a profile picture, the image is stored securely on our cloud storage infrastructure to display across your profile.
  • User Settings & Preferences: Local preferences such as theme (Light/Dark/System Default), language preference, notification toggles, and App Lock settings.

B. Files, Scanned Images, and Documents (Processed Locally)

  • Document Files: When you view, create, edit, convert, merge, split, or compress files (PDF, Word DOCX/DOC, Excel XLSX/XLS, PowerPoint PPTX/PPT, CSV, and TXT), all processing occurs entirely on your device.
  • Scanned Images & Camera Data: When using the Document Scanner or Scan-to-PDF tool:
    • Scanning is powered by Google Play services ML Kit Document Scanner and on-device machine learning.
    • Image processing (perspective cropping, color filters, grayscale, thresholding) runs locally.
    • We do NOT upload or transfer your documents, scanned images, or document contents to our servers.

C. Biometric Information (App Lock)

  • If you enable the "App Lock" feature, the App uses your device's native biometric authentication (fingerprint or face unlock) via standard Android system APIs.
On-Device Biometric Verification

DocHive never has access to, collects, or stores your biometric data. The biometric verification is managed exclusively by your device operating system.

3 Permissions Used by the App

DocHive requests only necessary permissions to deliver its functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with cloud authentication servers (Supabase and Google Sign-In), sync profile data, upload profile photos, and open support/help resources.
  • Storage / Storage Access Framework (SAF): We use Android's native system file picker (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT) to allow you to select documents. We only access the specific files you explicitly choose to open or import.
  • Camera / Scanner Interface: Used on-demand through Google Play services Document Scanner to capture physical documents. Images captured are converted locally to PDF.

4 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  1. Authentication & Session Management: To create and maintain your account, authenticate sign-ins, and restore active sessions.
  2. Profile Personalization: To display your name, email, and avatar in the App.
  3. App Functionality: To organize your favorite files, maintain a recents history, manage draft templates, and support local recycle bin recovery.
  4. Customer Support: To respond to your questions or support inquiries when you contact us.

5 Third-Party Service Providers

To operate certain features, we integrate trusted third-party services that may process information under strict data protection terms:

  1. Google Identity Services & Google Play Services:
    • Used for Google Sign-In authentication and on-device document scanning (ML Kit).
    • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  2. Supabase (Backend as a Service):
    • Used for user authentication (Auth) and storing user profile photos (Storage).
    • Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

6 Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • Data Security: All network communications between DocHive and our backend services are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Local Storage: Your document library metadata, favorites, and settings are saved securely on your device using Android app-isolated storage (SQLite/Room and Jetpack DataStore).
  • Data Retention:
    • Account and profile data are retained as long as your account remains active.
    • Local documents remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the App.

7 Account Deletion and User Data Rights

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations:

  • Account Deletion: You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data (such as email, name, and profile photo).
  • How to Request Deletion:
    • You can request account deletion by emailing us at support@dochive.app (or via our support portal: https://dochive.app/support) with the subject line "Request Account Deletion".
    • Upon receiving your verified request, we will permanently delete your account and associated cloud data from our authentication and storage servers within 30 days.
  • Local Data Removal: You can clear local app data at any time by navigating to your device's Settings > Apps > DocHive > Storage > Clear Data, or by using the in-app "Log Out" option which clears your local session and preferences.

8 Children’s Privacy

DocHive does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under the relevant age, we will immediately delete that information from our servers.

9 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
